Running a night

  1. Open the Google Sheet. Right-click a TEMPLATE tab, pick Duplicate, and rename the copy (example: 9-16 Auction 2).
  2. Fill in the yellow boxes: the box you're pulling from, the price, and the hits (prize, odds, picture link).
  3. Go to the BOARDS tab and type that exact tab name into LIVE TAB for the right board. The board on stream fades over to the new game on its own.
  4. During the game, type owners (and bids for auctions) into the SPOTS rows. Leave the finished tab alone as your record for the night.

Putting a board on stream

  1. Click OBS link on a board above. That copies an address that hides every button and message.
  2. In OBS add a Browser Source, paste the link. Use 1920 x 1080 for a wide scene, or 1080 x 1920 for Whatnot / TikTok (the board switches to the tall layout on its own).
  3. Running two of the same board at once? Add a row in BOARDS like auction-2, then add ?board=auction-2 to the end of that board's address.
